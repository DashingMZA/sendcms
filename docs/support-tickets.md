# Support tickets

Support works through tickets — one thread per issue, with replies
from you and from support staff. There is no public contact form for
support and no anonymous submission: you must be signed in, and
tickets are created from the dashboard only.

## Opening a ticket

Go to **Support** in the dashboard (`/dashboard/support`) and open a
new ticket (`/dashboard/support/new`). Describe the problem and what
you already tried — the more specific the report (which form, which
page, what you expected vs what happened), the faster the first
reply. One issue per ticket: if two unrelated things are broken,
open two tickets.

## Tracking a ticket

The Support list shows every ticket you opened with its status:

| Status                | Meaning                                                        |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `OPEN`                | Received, waiting for support to pick it up                    |
| `IN_PROGRESS`         | Support is working on it                                       |
| `WAITING_ON_CUSTOMER` | Support asked you something — reply so work can continue       |
| `RESOLVED`            | Support considers it fixed — check it, and say so if it is not |
| `CLOSED`              | Done — the thread is closed                                    |

Open a ticket to read the full thread and reply in it. Replies you
send are visible to support staff assigned to the ticket; staff can
reply, change the status, and assign the ticket to each other.

## What support can and cannot do

Support staff do full ticket work: reply, change status, and assign
tickets. They cannot see your API keys or your BYOK keys, and they
cannot change your plan or billing — billing changes go through
[Billing](billing.md) or the platform admin.
