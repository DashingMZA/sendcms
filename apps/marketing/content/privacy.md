title: "Privacy Policy"
lastUpdated: "Last updated: November 26, 2026"
sections:
  - title: "1. What SendCMS is"
    body:
      - 'SendCMS is a contact-form backend. Website owners ("you") register domains, create forms, and embed a script tag on their own websites. Visitors to those websites ("your visitors") fill in the forms, and their submissions are delivered to you in a dashboard inbox and by email.'
      - "This policy explains what data the service collects, why, and what happens to it. It covers the SendCMS service at sendcms.bond. If you run your own self-hosted copy of SendCMS, this policy does not apply to that copy — your own policies do."
  - title: "2. Data we collect from account holders"
    body:
      - "When you create an account, we collect your email address and your password, which is stored hashed, never in plain text. If you sign in with Google, GitHub, or Facebook, we receive the name and email address that provider shares for sign-in, and nothing else from that provider."
      - "We also store what you create in the service: your domains and their verification records, your forms and their fields, your notification settings, and your API keys. API keys are stored as hashes — the full key is shown to you once, when it is created, and never again."
      - "To enforce plan limits we keep counts of your AI and API (MCP) usage. These counts record how much you used, not the content of your submissions."
  - title: "3. Data we collect from your visitors"
    body:
      - "When a visitor submits one of your forms, we receive exactly what the form asks for — typically a name, an email address, and a message — plus the domain the submission came from and the time it was sent. We do not ask visitors for anything your form does not ask for."
      - "Form submissions belong to the people who wrote them, and you are responsible for handling them lawfully (see section 5 of the Terms of Service). SendCMS processes submissions only to deliver them to you: into your inbox and, if you enabled it, to your email."
      - "The form script stores nothing in your visitors' browsers — no cookies, no local storage, no tracking pixels. The marketing site at sendcms.bond runs no analytics and no third-party trackers."
  - title: "4. Cookies"
    body:
      - "When you sign in, we set one session cookie so the dashboard and API know who you are. It lasts 30 days and is shared across the sendcms.bond subdomains (dashboard, api, admin) so sign-in works everywhere. It contains only a session identifier — no personal data."
      - "We set no advertising or tracking cookies, and no cookies at all for visitors who only fill in a form."
  - title: "5. How we use your data"
    body:
      - "To run the service: deliver submissions to your inbox, send notification emails, and enforce your plan's limits."
      - "To keep accounts secure: verify your email address, send password-reset links, and let you sign in with an OAuth provider."
      - "To send email, we pass the message to our email provider — Resend, or the SMTP server configured for the service. The provider sees the recipient address and the message content, because that is what sending an email requires."
      - "We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else except as described here or when the law requires it."
  - title: "6. How long we keep data, and deletion"
    body:
      - "Submissions stay in your inbox until you delete them. You can delete a form or a domain at any time from the dashboard, which deletes its submissions with it."
      - "If you ask for your account to be erased, an admin reviews the request and, once approved, your account is anonymised: your email address is replaced, and your domains, forms, submissions, API keys, AI and MCP settings, and notification records are deleted. Usage counts are deleted with the account. We keep only a record that the erasure took place, without your email address or your content."
      - "Backups, if any exist, are overwritten on their normal cycle; erased data is not restored from them."
  - title: "7. Your choices and rights"
    body:
      - "You can see and change your account details, domains, forms, and notification settings at any time from the dashboard. You can delete your forms and domains, and you can ask for your account to be erased as described above."
      - "Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal data — for example to access it, correct it, or have it deleted. Contact us and we will help."
  - title: "8. Changes to this policy"
    body:
      - "SendCMS is in beta and this policy may change as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date."
  - title: "9. Contact"
    body:
      - "Questions about this policy, or about your data in SendCMS, can be sent through the contact form on this site."
